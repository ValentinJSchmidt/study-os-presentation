\section*{1. Initial expectation}

My initial expectation of the course was that it would be a practical, hands-on course where we build super complex and large systems, with a hard defined goal from the professor. 
I've expected tasks like building a full stack AI Agent with a custom orchestration and memory layer. When we in the beginning talked about finding the right project and  the right problem to solve, I was a bit confused. I tried to understand the reasoning behind this, and suddenly it clicked. Nowadays its not so much anymore about the
how, but more about the what you are building. I've heard a lot of podcasts and fire side chats of startup founders which said, that their biggest task was to find a problem which is actually needed and not a problem which they thought was a problem. 
My biggest learning in this course was the actual process of finding a problem, which is worth solving by actually interviewing the user group, designing surveys and analyzing the results.

\section*{3. Role and contribution in the group}
In my opinion our group worked in a really balanced and efficient way. Since all of us have different backgrounds and different skill sets, 
we were able to learn a lot from each other while progressing fast. 
My biggest contribution to the group was my technical knowledge and experience in building and prototyping applications. During the process

developed ideas how to improve the tool. His major contribution was the pivot into the no-db solution which was a huge improvement in terms of 
maintainability. Overall I think we worked really well together and complemented each other in a great way.
